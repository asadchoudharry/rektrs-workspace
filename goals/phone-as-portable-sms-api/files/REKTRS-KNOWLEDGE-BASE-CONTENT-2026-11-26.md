## Source 1 — Rektrs — Company Profile

Rektrs (Private) Limited is a technology and digital transformation company — "Your connected technology & growth partner." It helps organizations improve operations, customer experience and decision-making through practical technology. Its capabilities span AI and business automation, ERP, software engineering, cloud, cybersecurity, managed IT, digital growth and transformation strategy.

Rektrs works as a one-window service provider and 360° digital growth partner: it starts with the client's business goals, workflows and constraints, then designs, builds and supports connected systems through one coordinated, accountable delivery model.

Founder & CEO: Asad Choudhary. Rektrs was founded in 2020. Company references: SECP 0168453 · D-U-N-S 983893253.

Rektrs serves organizations across Pakistan, Saudi Arabia, the wider GCC and remote international markets, through a hybrid delivery model that connects remote-first engineering with onsite execution hubs in Pakistan and Saudi Arabia.

As published on rektrs.com: 9+ years of experience, 600+ projects delivered, 98% customer satisfaction.

Rektrs is a verified Google Cloud Partner (listed in the Google Cloud Partner directory), with a focus on Google Workspace co-sell and services.

Published case studies on rektrs.com include: AMAC Digital Transformation — a connected industrial business platform combining the corporate website, Saudi ERP and invoicing workflows, branded proposals and sales automation; Synapse School Management System — a connected school ERP, LMS and student information platform bringing student records, household fees, front-office operations and tenant governance into one system; and the JIS Corporate Digital Platform — corporate identity and business onboarding for a Saudi industrial services company, spanning website, sales collateral, business email and policy documentation.

## Source 3 — Rektrs — Products & Platforms

Rektrs builds and runs its own products alongside client work:

- **Operqon** — Rektrs' own cloud ERP platform for running business operations.
- **Sendqon** — Rektrs' business messaging platform (sendqon.rektrs.com): send and receive WhatsApp and SMS, run campaigns, manage a shared team inbox, and automate replies with flows and AI agents.
- **Postqon (Rektrs Social)** — a social media scheduling and publishing workspace (social.rektrs.com): prepare content, organize posts in a publishing calendar, connect permitted social channels, and schedule or publish from one place.
- **School Management System (SMS)** — Rektrs' proprietary system for schools: student records, household fees, front-office operations and school administration in one connected platform.
- **Learning Management System (LMS)** — Rektrs' proprietary learning platform for schools and training organizations, working alongside the School Management System.
- **School apps** — mobile apps for schools (parents, students and staff) connected to the school systems.
