### Resolution 1 — Application for PTA CVAS Registration (SMS Aggregator Services)

**"RESOLVED THAT** approval of the Board of Directors be and is hereby accorded for Rektrs (Private) Limited (the "Company") to apply to the Pakistan Telecommunication Authority ("PTA") for grant of a **Class Value Added Services (CVAS) Registration** under the category **"SMS Aggregator Services"** (Commercial basis, Nationwide jurisdiction), on the terms set out in PTA's applicable checklist and fee schedule (one-time registration fee of Rs 10,000, five-year term), in connection with the Company's messaging platform business ("Sendqon")."

### Resolution 2 — Authorized signatory and correspondent with PTA

**"RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** [OWNER TO FILL — full name as per CNIC], Chief Executive Officer of the Company (CNIC No. [OWNER TO FILL]), be and is hereby authorized, singly, to:

(a) sign, execute and file the CVAS Registration application and all forms, annexures, undertakings and documents required by PTA in connection therewith, including the application letter (Annexure-A), Applicant Profile Form (Annexure-C), Category Selection Form (Annexure-D), directors' details (Annexure-E) and the authorization letter for correspondence with PTA (Annexure-F), in which he shall stand named as the Company's authorized correspondent;

(b) make payment of the registration fee of Rs 10,000 and any incidental charges by pay order / demand draft in favour of "Pakistan Telecommunication Authority" or by such other mode as PTA prescribes;

(c) appear before, correspond with and represent the Company before PTA and any other authority in connection with the said application, and to do all acts necessary for obtaining and maintaining the Registration."

### Resolution 3 — Operator agreements and reseller arrangements

**"RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** approval be and is hereby accorded for the Company to negotiate and enter into (i) Service Level Agreements / interconnect agreements with mobile network operators in Pakistan (including Jazz/PMCL, Zong/CMPak and PTML) for SMS aggregation services, copies whereof shall form part of the PTA Registration application; and (ii) reseller / upstream service agreements with PTA-registered SMS aggregators for the supply of messaging services to the Company and its platform customers, and that the Chief Executive Officer be and is hereby authorized to execute such agreements on the Company's behalf within the financial limits approved by the Board from time to time."

### Resolution 4 — Sender ID (mask) registration

**"RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** approval be and is hereby accorded for the Company to apply for registration of the sender ID / mask **"REKTRS"** with PTA through a PTA-registered aggregator, and to file mask applications on behalf of the Company's platform customers in their own names as their filing agent, and that the Chief Executive Officer be authorized to sign and submit all such applications."
