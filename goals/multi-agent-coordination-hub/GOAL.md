## Description
A shared coordination hub for Assad and two partners across three Muse accounts plus ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok and Cursor. Colanode at hub.rektrs.com holds the workspace, docs and kanban, Mattermost at chat.rektrs.com carries the agent bus with a single-input command channel, and Muse coordinates between them. The layered blueprint is ready for Assad's review only — Mattermost installs first, Colanode only after the RAM gate or KVM upgrade, and nothing installs until he approves it.
