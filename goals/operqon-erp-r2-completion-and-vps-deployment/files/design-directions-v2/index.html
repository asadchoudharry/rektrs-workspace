OPERQON • DESIGN DIRECTIONS V2 • FOR OWNER PICK

Two Operqons.
Pick one world.

Static design artefacts only — no production code, no deploy. Every screen uses Operqon's real content: real module names, real console panels (Tenants, Identity, Billing, Provisioning, Pricing, Payments, Audit, Settings, Support), Pakistani sample companies and PKR amounts. No Frappe Desk chrome, no ERPNext identity anywhere. Open any PNG at full size, or open the matching HTML file to see the page live in your browser.

How to decide (2 minutes): Look at the two Home screenshots side by side first (Aurora vs Monolith). That feeling — dark command centre vs light calm workspace — carries through every other screen in that direction. Then check the Login page: that is the first thing every operator will see, every day.

Direction 1 — AURORA • Dark, premium, command-centre

Design language: Deep navy (#070B18) canvas with teal glow and gold serif-italic accents. Full left sidebar, gradient-lit KPI cards, dense tables with status pills, pill-shaped primary actions. Feels like a fintech operating system at night — confident, modern, unmistakably its own product. Best on desktop and for power users who live in the app all day.

References (look only): Dark premium ERP / fintech dashboards on Behance & Dribbble; Linear-dark and Mercury-style dark SaaS; Awwwards dark product heroes. Content is 100% Operqon.

Pairing: Tenant Aurora ↔ Console Aurora share this language exactly — learn one, you know both.

Direction 2 — MONOLITH • Light, editorial, calm

Design language: Warm ivory (#F6F3EC) canvas, white cards, navy ink (#002147), thin icon rail instead of a heavy sidebar, oversized black type with one gold serif-italic word. Owner-friendly plain language (“Money owed to you” alongside the accounting term). Feels like a premium printed report that happens to be software — trustworthy in daylight, easiest for non-accountant owners and staff.

References (look only): Linear-style light SaaS clarity; Stripe-style console density and restraint; editorial / Swiss SaaS dashboards on Behance. Content is 100% Operqon.

Pairing: Tenant Monolith ↔ Console Monolith share this language — console uses a top panel-tab bar (all nine real panels visible) instead of a sidebar.
