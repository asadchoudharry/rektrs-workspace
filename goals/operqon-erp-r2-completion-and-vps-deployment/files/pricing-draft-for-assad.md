## Trial & policies (proposed)

- **Trial:** 3 days (set by Assad, 2026-11-06), full Growth features, no
  card required. On expiry the site becomes read-only until a plan is
  paid (no data deleted for 30 days).
- **Billing:** monthly or yearly, via hosted checkout (Stripe / JazzCash /
  Easypaisa / PayFast as they go live) or manual bank transfer (stays
  PENDING until the billing approver verifies the credit — already the
  R2 rule).
- **Cancellation:** cancel anytime; access continues to the end of the
  paid period; no partial-period refunds on monthly, pro-rata credit on
  yearly if cancelled within the first 30 days.
- **Taxes:** prices shown exclusive of sales tax; tax applied per the
  customer's province/status at invoice time. *(Decided by Assad
  2026-11-06: tax-excluded.)*
